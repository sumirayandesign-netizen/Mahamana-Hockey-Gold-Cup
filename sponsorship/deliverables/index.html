PACKAGE DETAILS

What each package can include.

A readable detailed breakdown of each proposed tier.

₹15,00,000

Powered By / Title Sponsor

Primary naming rights, lead branding on approved event materials and apparel, digital recognition and a ceremonial opportunity.

  • Naming rights (subject to agreement)
  • Lead venue and jersey visibility
  • Website, media and campaign branding
  • 20 VIP passes and 2-minute remarks
  • Winner or best-player felicitation opportunity
₹10,00,000

Platinum Sponsor

High-priority visual branding with apparel, on-ground signage and one award-presentation opportunity.

  • Second-tier logo priority
  • Selected jersey sleeve branding
  • Digital acknowledgements
  • 10 VIP passes
  • Full-page advertisement in media kit
₹6,50,000

Food & Refreshment Partner

Hospitality zone, meal coupons and food-service recognition throughout the event.

  • Official Hospitality Partner designation
  • Food-counter and meal-coupon visibility
  • Social and website mentions
  • 6 VIP passes
  • Fair Play Award presentation opportunity
₹5,00,000

Co-Sponsor

A flexible way to be visible across event communication, print and digital promotion.

  • Event promotional branding
  • Website and social acknowledgement
  • 4 VIP passes
  • Ceremony recognition
  • Half-page media-kit advertisement
₹3,00,000 · cash or kind

Happy Journey / Transport Partner

Transport-related branding for teams and officials, subject to approved movement plans.

  • Approved transport vehicle branding
  • Pick-up/drop signage
  • Transport help-desk recognition
  • 4 VIP passes
  • Consolation-award opportunity
₹1,00,000

Lanyard & Badge Sponsor

Distinctive high-frequency identity on event lanyards and identification cards.

  • Exclusive official ID/lanyard branding
  • Sponsor board listing
  • Website recognition
  • Social acknowledgement
In kind

Official Gifting Partner

Curated gifts and tokens for players, officials, umpires and distinguished attendees.

  • Gifting material brand visibility
  • Approved event backdrop placement
  • Website acknowledgement
  • Half-page media-kit advertisement
