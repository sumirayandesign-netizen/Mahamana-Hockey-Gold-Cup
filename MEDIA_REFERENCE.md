## Instagram

Nineteen **unique** official Instagram links were supplied (one pasted link was a duplicate). They are stored in `content/site-content.json` under `reels`. The website attempts to show Instagram's public embed on click, and always presents the original post link as a fallback. Instagram may restrict playback because of login, region, third-party cookies, removed posts or account settings. These limitations are outside the website's control.
