2027 — The Next Chapter

The second Mahamana Hockey Gold Cup is scheduled for 15, 16 and 17 January 2027 at the International Hockey Stadium, Rajgir. The championship is planned for Under-16 boys’ and girls’ teams.

THE ROAD TO RAJGIR

A stronger next chapter.

A championship built on the success of the inaugural event.

Following its January 2026 debut, Mahamana Hockey Gold Cup returns with the ambition to create stronger exposure for emerging hockey players. It is conceived as a setting in which technical skill, courage, collaboration and commitment can be recognized.

Both boys’ and girls’ teams remain at the heart of the competition. Match planning, schedules, eligibility documentation and participation will be updated as they are officially finalized.
