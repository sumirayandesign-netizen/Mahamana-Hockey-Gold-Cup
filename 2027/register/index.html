Team Enquiry — Under-16 · 2027

Interested academies and teams may contact the organizing team. Official registration dates, player eligibility criteria, team quotas and age-verification process have not yet been announced.

Interested in taking part?

We welcome expressions of interest from coaches, academy managers and adult team coordinators across India. An enquiry does not guarantee a tournament place; approved team selection and formal registration will follow official guidelines.

For athlete safety and privacy

Do not send children’s identity documents, medical information, dates of birth, photographs or player rosters through this public form. Official verification instructions will be communicated separately when registration opens.

Does an enquiry register my team?

No. It helps the organizing team respond to interested institutions. Registration is confirmed only via an official process.

Which categories are planned?

Under-16 boys and under-16 girls. The final age eligibility cutoff is subject to official event rules.

How will we receive a reply?

The organizing team may reply through the representative’s email or phone once the form receiving service is activated.
