OUR PURPOSE

A tournament with a purpose.

The Gold Cup gives talented young players, particularly those with fewer pathways to the spotlight, the chance to experience high-level inter-state competition.

Inspired by the educational and social ideals of Mahamana Pandit Madan Mohan Malviya, the tournament links sporting opportunity with discipline, determination and teamwork. Its first chapter began in Rajgir in January 2026 — and the journey continues in 2027.

THE FOUR PILLARS

More than a championship.

A youth-focused initiative where sport becomes a doorway to confidence, character and opportunity.

01

Spot hidden talent

Create opportunities for promising young players whose talent deserves wider recognition.

02

Build Bihar’s pathways

Encourage exposure, confidence and competitive experience for players from Bihar.

03

Celebrate equal opportunity

Recognise both the boys’ and girls’ categories with equal competitive respect.

04

Compete on a major stage

Offer young athletes the opportunity to play at the International Hockey Stadium, Rajgir.
